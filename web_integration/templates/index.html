ABOUT THE PROJECT

Olympic Games Performance Analytics

Comprehensive interactive visualizations dissecting multi-decade Olympic performance, medal distribution, regional efficiency, and sport-specific outcomes.

📊 Comprehensive Data

Integrates 4 unified datasets covering 8,000 athlete profiles, 910 national medal results, 60 all-time country rankings, and 18 Summer & Winter Games summaries (1992–2026).

🌍 Global Coverage

Evaluates 60 National Olympic Committees (NOCs) across 7 geographic regions and 21 distinct sport categories to benchmark regional efficiency.

🥇 Medal Intelligence

Deep-dives into Gold, Silver, and Bronze distributions, per-athlete yields (Rowing at 6.916, Shooting at 6.816), and sports investment returns.

Data Insights

🇺🇸 USA & China Dominance

The United States leads overall with 735 total medals (250 Gold), closely followed by China with 696 total medals (230 Gold).

🚣 High-Yield Sport Categories

Rowing (6.916) and Shooting (6.816) generate the highest average medals per athlete across the 21 sport categories.

🌎 Regional Efficiency Leaders

South America leads regional medal efficiency at 49.64%, followed by Europe/Asia at 45.98% and Africa at 42.80%.
